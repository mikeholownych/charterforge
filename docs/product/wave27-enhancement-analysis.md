## Detailed Specifications

### 1. Autonomous Real-Time Capital Re-Balancing Autopilot
* **Context:** Trailing projects trap corporate capital that could accelerate high-yielding growth bets.
* **Intervention:**
  1. Add `auto_rebalance_departmental_capital_allocation(conn, organization_id)` in [finance_db.py](file:///home/mike/Projects/hermes-agent/hermes_cli/finance_db.py).
  2. Reallocates unspent budget allocations based on verified objective net yield.

### 2. Autonomous Competitive Counter-Strategy Dispatcher
* **Context:** Competitor market moves demand instant, non-delaying strategic counter-actions.
* **Intervention:**
  1. Add `dispatch_competitive_counter_strategy(conn, organization_id, competitor_name="RivalSaaS", signal_type="price_cut")` in [business_metrics.py](file:///home/mike/Projects/hermes-agent/hermes_cli/business_metrics.py).
  2. Switches strategy routes and updates pricing policies automatically.

### 3. Autonomous Board Resolution Auto-Execution Engine
* **Context:** Approved board resolutions must immediately translate into operational mandates without administrative friction.
* **Intervention:**
  1. Add `auto_execute_approved_board_resolutions(conn, organization_id, resolution_id)` in [organization_db.py](file:///home/mike/Projects/hermes-agent/hermes_cli/organization_db.py).
  2. Converts resolution items into active mandates, budgets, and worker credentials.

### 4. Autonomous Cross-Departmental Dependency Resolver
* **Context:** Sequential multi-department objectives frequently stall on unassigned upstream dependencies.
* **Intervention:**
  1. Add `resolve_cross_departmental_objective_dependencies(conn, organization_id)` in [operational_control.py](file:///home/mike/Projects/hermes-agent/hermes_cli/operational_control.py).
  2. Elevates priority and re-allocates worker swarms to clear critical path bottlenecks.
